Muhammed Fayis is a PMP® Certified Project Planning and Management Engineer based in Dammam, Saudi Arabia, bringing over five years of multidisciplinary expertise in project management, planning, execution, and scheduling.

His core competencies lie in end-to-end multidisciplinary coordination, baseline schedule development via Primavera P6, rigorous QA/QC documentation compliance, engineering change orders management, proactive resource management, and critical Extension of Time (EOT) claims substantiation.
